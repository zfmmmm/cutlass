# SM80 AutoPartitioner Optimization Log

Baseline command: `build/auto_partitioner_bench/bin/sm80_autopartition_gemm --m=1024 --n=1024 --k=1024 --warmup=3 --iterations=10 --skip-reference`

## v00 baseline_single_stage

- Snapshot: `sm80_autopartition_gemm_v00_baseline_single_stage.cu`
- Change state: single-stage `cp.async -> wait<0> -> cooperative_gemm` mainloop, accumulator RF->SMEM->RF epilogue.
- Correctness: 128x128x128 max_abs_diff = 4.76837e-07.
- Perf command: `build/auto_partitioner_bench/bin/sm80_autopartition_gemm --m=1024 --n=1024 --k=1024 --warmup=3 --iterations=10 --skip-reference`
- Result: 0.060112 ms, 35.7247 TFLOP/s.

## v01 policy_tiled_g2s

- Snapshot: `sm80_autopartition_gemm_v01_policy_tiled_g2s.cu`
- Change from v00: A/B mainloop global-to-shared copies now use `PartA::GlobalToSharedCopy` and `PartB::GlobalToSharedCopy` through `get_thread_slice()` + `partition_S/D()` + `copy(...)`, instead of generic `cooperative_copy(..., PartA::GmemToSmemCopy{})`.
- Why it helps: uses the concrete tiled copy generated by AutoPartitioner policy, avoiding the generic cooperative-copy heuristic on the hot G2S path.
- Correctness: 128x128x128 max_abs_diff = 4.76837e-07.
- Perf command: `build/auto_partitioner_bench/bin/sm80_autopartition_gemm --m=1024 --n=1024 --k=1024 --warmup=3 --iterations=10 --skip-reference`
- Result: 0.0587936 ms, 36.5258 TFLOP/s.
- Improvement over v00: +0.8011 TFLOP/s, +2.24%.

## v02 hoist_g2s_tiled_copy

- Snapshot: `sm80_autopartition_gemm_v02_hoist_g2s_tiled_copy.cu`
- Change from v01: hoisted `PartA::GlobalToSharedCopy`, `PartB::GlobalToSharedCopy`, and their thread slices outside the K-tile loop.
- Why it helps: reduces loop-body work and gives the compiler a simpler hot path around `partition_S/D()` and `copy(...)`.
- Correctness: 128x128x128 max_abs_diff = 4.76837e-07.
- Perf command: `build/auto_partitioner_bench/bin/sm80_autopartition_gemm --m=1024 --n=1024 --k=1024 --warmup=3 --iterations=10 --skip-reference`
- Result: 0.0579072 ms, 37.0849 TFLOP/s.
- Improvement over v01: +0.5591 TFLOP/s, +1.53%.
- Improvement over v00: +1.3602 TFLOP/s, +3.81%.

## v03 launch_bounds

- Snapshot: `sm80_autopartition_gemm_v03_launch_bounds.cu`
- Change from v02: added `__launch_bounds__(ThreadCount, 2)` to the SM80 AutoPartitioner GEMM kernel.
- Why it helps: gives NVCC/PTXAS the intended occupancy contract for the 128-thread CTA and controls register allocation for at least 2 CTAs per SM.
- Correctness: 128x128x128 max_abs_diff = 4.76837e-07.
- Perf command: `build/auto_partitioner_bench/bin/sm80_autopartition_gemm --m=1024 --n=1024 --k=1024 --warmup=3 --iterations=10 --skip-reference`
- Result: 0.0574688 ms, 37.3678 TFLOP/s.
- Improvement over v02: +0.2829 TFLOP/s, +0.76%.
- Improvement over v00: +1.6431 TFLOP/s, +4.60%.

## v04 launch_bounds_3cta

- Snapshot: `sm80_autopartition_gemm_v04_launch_bounds_3cta.cu`

- Improvement over v03: +0.7536 TFLOP/s, +2.02%.

## v06 shared_union

- Snapshot: `sm80_autopartition_gemm_v06_shared_union.cu`
- Change from v05: changed `SharedStorage` to a union of `MainloopStorage{smemA, smemB}` and `EpilogueStorage{smemC}` because A/B shared memory and C accumulator staging are used in disjoint phases. Reverted the failed `128x64x64` tile experiment back to `64x64x64`.
- Why it helps: reduces the live static shared footprint for the 64x64x64 kernel from A+B+C combined storage to max(mainloop, epilogue), improving CTA residency/latency hiding while keeping the required RF->SMEM->RF epilogue path.
- Correctness: 128x128x128 max_abs_diff = 4.76837e-07.
- Perf command: `build/auto_partitioner_bench/bin/sm80_autopartition_gemm --m=1024 --n=1024 --k=1024 --warmup=3 --iterations=10 --skip-reference`
- Result: 0.0552512 ms, 38.8676 TFLOP/s.
- Improvement over v05: +0.4604 TFLOP/s, +1.20%.
- Improvement over v00: +3.1429 TFLOP/s, +8.80%.
